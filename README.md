## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is NurViewer free?

Yes. NurViewer provides an ongoing Free edition with no trial expiration.

Many everyday reading, viewing, annotation and conversion functions remain free.

Optional Standard and Pro upgrades provide additional capabilities and higher processing allowances.

[View current plans and feature limits](https://nursova.com/nurviewer/)

### Are my files uploaded to a server?

Ordinary local viewing, OCR, searching and conversion are performed on your device.

Account verification, licensing and usage allowances use online account services.

[Read our Privacy Policy](https://nursova.com/nurviewer/privacy/)

### Does NurViewer support every feature for every format?

No. The available functions depend on the file type, encoding, platform and document structure.

Some formats offer comprehensive reading tools, while others provide basic preview or metadata inspection.

Please test your own files using the Free edition.

### Is NurViewer open source?

NurViewer is proprietary commercial software.

This public repository provides documentation, official download links, technical information, bug reporting and community support.

The application source code is not included.

## Help Us Test Difficult Files

Our development team is particularly interested in challenging real-world file samples.

We welcome reports involving:

- EPUB files that other readers cannot open
- Complex EPUB layouts and vertical text
- EPUB-to-PDF conversion failures
- Unusually slow Calibre conversions
- Difficult PDG and CAJ documents
- Damaged PDF files
- Complex OCR samples
- Tables that are difficult to recognize
- Corrupted images and archives
- Unusual file-format compatibility problems

We investigate selected compatibility and file-recovery cases to improve NurViewer.

Where possible, we may help recover or convert problematic files free of charge as part of our testing and research.

Successful recovery cannot be guaranteed.

Please contact us privately before sending confidential or copyrighted material.
